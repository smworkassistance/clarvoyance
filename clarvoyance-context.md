# Clarvoyance — App Purpose & Tool Context

## Core Philosophy
The entire app is built on one belief: **the way you feel is everything.**
Feelings attract reality — the incidents, people, and experiences in our life are a reflection of our dominant feeling state. All tools in Clarvoyance use the user's current feeling as the basis for analysis and guidance.

---

## How Do I Feel (Mood Selection)
Mood detection is the foundation of the app. The user selects their current feeling because feeling attracts reality. Every tool and recommendation stems from this emotional baseline. The mood score (0–100) reflects the quality of the user's current vibrational state.

---

## Non-Negotiables
Practices, routines, or habits that cannot be negotiated at any cost. These are must-do actions decided by the user — daily, weekly, or at whatever frequency they choose. Once committed to, they are non-negotiable. They represent the minimum standard the user holds for themselves.

---

## Goals
The user's present desires and goals they are actively striving for. Goals must be:
- Revised regularly
- Repeated consciously
- Reinforced in the mind
The purpose is to keep the goal alive in the user's awareness so it can be attracted into reality.

---

## Vibe Feed
Random practices, tools, and challenges designed to shift the user's focus and move them toward a better, more focused feeling place. The feed is unpredictable (like reels) to keep engagement high while each card serves a specific emotional shift purpose.

---

## Tools
A collection of guided tools that help the user reach a better feeling place or a clearer mental state. Each tool has a specific purpose:

### T1 — Written Clarity
A space to write and discuss any ongoing issue with yourself, or brainstorm to think more clearly. Externalising thoughts reduces mental noise and creates clarity.

### T2 — What I Don't Want
When you know what you don't want, you discover what you do want. Writing what is making you feel bad, what you want to avoid — creates clarity about the desired direction.

### T3 — New Story
Your life is as good as the story you tell about it. Write a new story right now — the reality you expect to have — so you can begin to attract it. Rewriting your narrative shifts your vibration.

### T4 — Find Better Feeling
The way you feel is everything. This tool guides the user to consciously find a better feeling place — not just emotionally, but vibrationally. Shifting feeling = shifting life experience.

### T5 — The Culprit
This tool focuses on a hard truth: you are the only one responsible for your life experience. No external person or event has the power to disturb your life unless you give them that power. Running away from this responsibility keeps you stuck. The tool guides the user to reclaim their power.

---

## Revise and Repeat
Contains images, charts, quotes, and thoughts that form the philosophical foundation of this app. Users come here to revise and repeat the core concepts in order to sharpen their perspective and keep the philosophy alive in their daily thinking.

---

## Focus Timer
NOT a standalone productivity tool. It exists purely as a focus aid — to help the user stay undistracted while using any tool inside the app. It should be available as a supporting utility, not as a primary action or quest goal.

---

## Ideas (Quick Capture)
A lightweight notepad for spontaneous inspired ideas that come to mind at any moment. Not a major feature — just a quick way to capture a thought before it disappears. No structured input required.

---

## Notes for Future Development
- Whenever new tools are added, their context and purpose will be documented here.
- All quests, vibe cards, and features should align with the core philosophy: **feeling is everything.**
- Focus Timer and Ideas are utility features — never make them quest goals.
- Low mood → T2 (What I Don't Want), T4 (Find Better Feeling), T1 (Written Clarity)
- Mid mood → Non-Negotiables, Vibe Feed, Goals (revisit/reinforce)
- High mood → T3 (New Story), Goal update, Revise & Repeat, T5 (The Culprit)
