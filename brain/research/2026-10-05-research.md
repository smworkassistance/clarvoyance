## Question 1 — Dark and light mode for a professional dashboard
- [FACT] WCAG 2.x Success Criterion 1.4.3 (W3C): body text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, and large text at least 3:1. Applies to both themes. Source: W3C WCAG 2.1, https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/ (standard text, not fetched in this pass).
- [INFERENCE] Pure black (#000) backgrounds with bright text cause glare for many readers. Dark gray (for example #121212) is the common practice in design guidance. Source: https://uxcel.com/blog/12-principles-of-dark-mode-design-627 (a design blog; treat as guidance, not a standard).
- [INFERENCE] Saturated colors often fail contrast on dark backgrounds, so accent colors need a lighter variant in dark mode. Source: https://www.accessibilitychecker.org/blog/dark-mode-accessibility/ (guidance).
- Decision applied in the dashboard: system theme by default, a manual toggle, text contrast designed to meet 4.5:1 in both themes, visible focus outlines, no pure black.

## Question 2 — How strong is the evidence for color and emotion (C-13)
- [FACT] A systematic review of 132 empirical studies (1895–2022, 42,266 participants, 64 countries) found real, documented links between colors and emotions. Source: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12325498/
- [FACT] The same literature shows many-to-many, context-dependent links, not one fixed meaning per color. Example from the review: happiness was linked to 8 color categories. Same source.
- [FACT] The field has uneven experimental rigor, so popular "red means X, blue means Y" claims are weaker than the review's overall finding. Same source.
- Evidence label for the owner's color idea: **strong that color affects mood, weak for any single fixed color meaning.** Do not present a specific color as "the" answer for a feeling.

## Question 3 — What product mechanics drive daily habit (world-class example)
- [FACT] Duolingo's streak counter is a documented, visible daily-habit mechanic, and Duolingo has published about its streak features. Source: https://desirabilitylab.com/backfill/backfill-2025-46-duolingo-streak-design (a design analysis).
- [UNVERIFIED] "20–40% DAU lift" from streaks, and "the single most effective retention lever". These come from secondary blog text with no primary source. Do not use these numbers.
- [INFERENCE] For Clar, the transferable part is that a visible, forgiving streak (Duolingo adds streak freezes) lowers the cost of missing one day. Clar already has a weekly freeze in its streak design (CLAUDE.md v125), so this is a check of an existing choice, not a new feature.
