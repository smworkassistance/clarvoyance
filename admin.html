📐 Formulas — reference only, matches the code exactly, for understanding what the numbers above actually do
per section (vibe / chat / fortune / goal / home / self): time_ratio = min(1, actual_minutes ÷ target_minutes) xp_ratio = min(1, actual_xp ÷ target_xp) section_score = (time_ratio + xp_ratio) ÷ 2 across all 6 sections, once per day: avg_section = average(6 section_scores) nn_multiplier = full_multiplier if NN% ≥ full_threshold partial_multiplier if NN% ≥ partial_threshold none_multiplier otherwise daily_score = avg_section × nn_multiplier (0 to ~1) momentum growth (once per day, replaces the old flat "+1"): momentum = momentum + daily_score momentum decay (continuous, real HOURS since the last day that crossed the engaged-day threshold — not whole calendar days): pct_retained = exp(-decay_k × hours_since_engaged ^ decay_exponent) momentum = momentum × pct_retained

Checks, for one specific person, whether the app's core belief actually holds in their real data: do the days that followed a higher-Clar-engagement stretch (their own last 3 days, recency-weighted — older history is treated as less predictive, per the idea that mood shifts with Clar usage fairly fast) get a better emotional read from Clar than the days that followed a quieter stretch? Needs at least 6 real days with both a daily_score and a logged state_read on record — below that it'll say so rather than guess.
