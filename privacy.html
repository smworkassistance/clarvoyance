Clar ("we", "the app") is a self-development app available at clar.co.in and as an Android app. This page explains what information Clar collects, why, and how you can control it.

1. Signing in with Google

Clar's "Continue with Google" button asks for only the standard information Google shares for sign-in — nothing more:

  • Your name — shown back to you in the app so it can greet you by name.
  • Your email address — used to identify your account and, if you contact us, to find your account.
  • Your Google profile photo — shown as your avatar in the app.

Clar does not ask for access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, contacts, or anything else in your Google account. Only the three items above are ever requested or received.

This information is used solely to run your Clar account as described on this page. It is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone for their own marketing purposes.

2. What Clar stores about you

Once you use the app (whether signed in with Google or not — Clar also works with a private, un-signed-in account), the following may be stored, tied to your account, so the app can work the way it's meant to (remembering your goals, your streak, your conversations with Clar):

  • Your conversations with Clar AI, and short daily summaries of them
  • Anything you write in Clar (goals, journal-style entries, "Non-Negotiables" checklist, personal reflections)
  • Photos you choose to upload (e.g. to a Goal or to the Community feature)
  • App usage data used to compute your streak/progress (e.g. time spent in each part of the app, which practices you complete)
  • If you join the optional Community feature: your chosen display name/handle, and anything you choose to post or share there (posts are only shown per your own chosen privacy settings)

This is stored in our database (Supabase, a secure cloud database provider) and, for uploaded photos/videos, in secure cloud file storage (Supabase Storage, and Bunny.net for Community videos). It is private to your account by default; only the parts you choose to make visible in the Community feature are shown to other users.

3. The Clar AI conversation

When you chat with Clar, your message is sent to Google's Gemini AI model (via our own server, so our access keys are never exposed to your device) to generate a reply. Google's own handling of that request is governed by Google's Gemini API terms. We do not use your conversations to train any AI model ourselves.

Clar is not a medical, psychiatric, or crisis service. If you are in crisis or need urgent help, please contact a licensed professional or a local helpline — Clar is a self-development companion, not a substitute for that.

4. Analytics

We use two analytics tools to understand how the app is used (which screens people open, whether something is confusing, general usage patterns) — never to identify you personally by name:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — event-level usage data (e.g. "a tool was opened").
  • Microsoft Clarity — session recordings and heatmaps of how people navigate the app, used to find and fix confusing parts of the interface.

5. Content features that don't involve your personal data

Parts of the app (goal-matched video suggestions, quote cards) fetch content from YouTube, Wikiquote, and (when configured) Pexels/Pixabay. These requests are about topics/interests you've expressed, not your identity — no personal data is sent to these services on your behalf.

6. Payments and subscriptions

Not live yet. Clar does not currently process any payments — there is no paid tier today. This section will be completed, with full detail on what's collected/shared with our payment provider (planned: Razorpay) and our refund policy, before any paid feature goes live.

7. Your choices

  • Sign out any time from the Profile tab.
  • Delete your Community profile (if you joined) from Community → You → Privacy — this removes your public Community presence, follows, and posts.
  • Delete your whole account and all stored data: this isn't yet a self-service button in the app — email or WhatsApp us (below) and we'll delete it for you. (Owner note: worth building a real in-app delete option before this scales — flagged, not blocking.)

8. Children

Clar is not directed at children under 13. The optional Community feature specifically requires an 18+ self-declaration to join.
