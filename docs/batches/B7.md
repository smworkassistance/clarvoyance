## Decisions fixed by the owner
- YouTube only (no self-hosted video); nothing that breaches YouTube policy: no overlays on the player, no title cropping; own UI *below* the player.
- Vibe feed keeps mixing videos with practices.
- Audio: once unmuted, stays on for following videos. Shared video shows as a playing inline player in the feed.
- Invite text (simple English, no claims): "I'm transforming my life with Clar. Let's grow together 🌱 What are the first few goals or desires you want to fulfil? Join me: <link>". Inviter +100 XP when the invitee signs in with Google and completes their first day; invitee +50 XP.
- XP every time a video passes 50% (cap 30 awards/day, admin-editable).
- Interaction XP: read (≥5 s in view) +1, like +1, save +2, share +3, cap 50/day (admin-editable).
- Guides: 5 starters (Clar Daily, Manifestation, Strategy & Wisdom, Business & Startups, Relationships & Inner Peace); user *intention* bots with a confirmed Blueprint; topic de-duplication ("join the existing one"); languages chosen by the user (English, Hindi, Hinglish, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati), content generated in that language lazily; all free for now (gates later); posts in the Feed tab, labelled "Guide".
- Safety: sources only (RSS/official feeds, Wikiquote, public-domain classics, YouTube embed); grounded generation + verifier pass + citation required; "AI-curated · general information, not advice — for specific help consult a qualified professional"; report flag (false info / harmful / spam / off-topic), auto-pause at 3 reports; scope guard (self-help/life-betterment only); distress keywords → gentle helpline note.
- App-start disclaimer (one-time): "Clar is not a substitute for a doctor or consultant. It helps you build a positive mindset and point it in the right direction."
- UI language stays English in this batch (only *content* is per-language).
